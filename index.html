Privacy Policy for Word Fusion

Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Fusion handles information when you use the Word Fusion mobile application on Android or iOS.

Information Word Fusion Stores

Word Fusion does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, password, or other account information in order to play.

Game information such as your progress, settings, streaks, completed levels, and locally generated Rival Board information may be stored on your device so the game can remember your progress and preferences.

The Rival Board is a simulated game feature. Rival names and scores shown there do not represent real online players or user accounts.

Advertising

Word Fusion uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising technologies may automatically process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate or general location derived from it
  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • Advertising interactions
  • App interactions, such as launches and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for purposes including advertising, analytics, measuring advertising performance, security, and fraud prevention.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information about Google's advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Privacy Choices and Consent

In regions where privacy laws require consent or privacy choices, Word Fusion may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present a privacy message before certain advertising technologies are used.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be given options to consent, decline consent, or manage individual privacy choices.

Word Fusion may also provide access to privacy options when required so that eligible users can review or change their choices.

Third-Party Services

Word Fusion currently uses Google AdMob and related Google Mobile Ads and privacy technologies to provide advertising and manage applicable advertising consent requirements.

These third-party services may collect and process information independently according to their own privacy policies.

Data We Do Not Request

Word Fusion does not require users to submit personal profile information to play the game.

Word Fusion does not currently operate user accounts, an online player-profile system, or a real-player leaderboard.

Children's Privacy

Word Fusion does not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other personal profile information from children through the game.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us using the address below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Word Fusion changes, including when new services or features are added. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
